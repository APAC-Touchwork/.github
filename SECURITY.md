## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public GitHub issue to report a security vulnerability.**

If you discover a security vulnerability in any of our repositories, please report it **privately** by emailing:

📧 **[security@apac-touchwork.com](mailto:security@apac-touchwork.com)**

### What to Include in Your Report

To help us triage and resolve the issue as quickly as possible, please include:

- A description of the vulnerability and the potential impact
- The affected repository and version(s)
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability
- Any proof-of-concept code, if available
- Your suggested fix or mitigation, if you have one

### What to Expect After Reporting

1. **Acknowledgement within 48 hours** — We will confirm receipt of your report.
2. **Initial assessment within 5 business days** — We will evaluate the severity and scope.
3. **Resolution timeline** — We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities within **14 days** and moderate/low vulnerabilities within **30–90 days**, depending on complexity.
4. **Disclosure** — We will coordinate with you on responsible disclosure once a fix is available.

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose security issues and will acknowledge your contribution if you wish.

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Do **not** open a public issue for a security vulnerability.
- ❌ Do **not** disclose the vulnerability publicly before we have had a chance to address it.
- ❌ Do **not** exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it.
